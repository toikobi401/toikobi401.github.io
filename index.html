Rift Arena

Rift Arena is a series of short videos showing 1v1 battles between stylized geometric fighters. Every match is fully simulated: nobody controls the fighters, and every result is recorded in a season ledger with Elo ratings.

Season 1 is a round robin of 16 fighters, released daily as short videos.

Rift Arena Publisher

Rift Arena Publisher is the private tool the channel owner uses to produce and post episodes. The owner reviews the candidate simulations for each episode, picks one, confirms the post settings, and the tool uploads that video to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to other users.
